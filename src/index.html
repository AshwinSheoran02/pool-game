How to play
  • Drag on the table to aim. Click or tap a ball to aim straight at it.
  • Pull the Power bar down and let go to shoot, or hold Space and release.
  • Use the Fine strip or Shift + arrow keys for tiny aim corrections.
  • Tap the cue ball face to add spin: top for follow, bottom for draw, sides for english.
  • Ball in hand: drag the white ball to any clear spot before shooting.
  • Pot your group (solids or stripes), then the 8. Potting the 8 early, or scratching on it, loses the frame.
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom. Press C to switch between the 45° view, top view and low view.
